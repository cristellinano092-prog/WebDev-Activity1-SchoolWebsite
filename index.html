K-POP,P-POP

Both K-pop (Korean Pop) and P-pop (Pinoy/Philippine Pop) are powerhouse music industries that feature highly trained idol groups, spectacular choreography, and dedicated global fandoms. While they share intense training structures, they differ uniquely in cultural roots, language, and sonic flavors.

ENHYPEN

ENHYPEN is a South Korean boy band formed by Belift Lab through the 2020 reality survival show I-LAND. The group debuted on November 30, 2020, with their first mini-album, Border: Day One. Their name comes from the hyphen symbol (-), which means different people connect, discover each other, and grow together. Current Members • Jungwon: The leader and vocalist of the group. • Jay: A dancer, rapper, and vocalist. • Jake: A vocalist and rapper. • Sunghoon: A vocalist and former competitive figure skater. • Sunoo: A vocalist known for his bright charm. • Ni-ki: The youngest member and main dancer from Japan.

BTS

BTS (also known as Bangtan Sonyeondan or the Bangtan Boys) is a globally legendary South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment (now BIGHIT MUSIC under HYBE). Debuting on June 13, 2013, they have risen from a small, budget-constrained agency to become the biggest musical group in the world, shattering countless global streaming, charting, and sales records

CORTIS

CORTIS (stylized in all caps) is a breakout five-member South Korean K-pop boy band formed by BigHit Music (HYBE), making them the label's third major boy group following global icons BTS and TOMORROW X TOGETHER. They officially debuted on August 18, 2025, with the digital single "What You Want".
